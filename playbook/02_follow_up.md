# 02 · Follow-up e-mail (data late or wrong format)

**When:** the sample or full drop is 2+ working days late, or the intake report shows a blocking check.
**Tone:** factual, specific, helpful. One ask per bullet. Give the impact on the timeline, not blame.

---

**Subject:** Evaluation – data drop: 2 fixes needed to keep the week-3 training date

Hi [engineering lead name],

Thanks for the file you sent on [date]. We ran our intake checks on it (report attached). Two points block training; everything else looks good:

1. **9.3 % of events point to items missing from the catalog** (49,815 item ids). Could you re-export the catalog **including sold and delisted listings** from [start date]?
2. **Time zone:** timestamps have no offset. Could you confirm they are UTC (or tell us the zone)?

Non-blocking, for later: 2 days have unusually low volume (17 and 18 Sept); was tracking reduced, or is the export partial?

**Impact on the plan:** if we have the corrected catalog by **[date, +3 working days]**, training still starts on [week 3 date]. After that, each day of delay moves the A/B start by one day.

Happy to jump on a 15-minute call if it is easier to go through it live. I am also copying [PM name] so the timeline stays visible on both sides.

Best,
[name]

---

*Escalation rule:* if there is still no answer after a second follow-up and 5 working days, raise it with the customer PM in the weekly check-in and flag the date risk in the weekly report (see `04_escalation_grid.md`).
